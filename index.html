Storytelling · Discussion

Holiday Stories

Eight visual prompts for sharing summer memories, building longer answers and asking natural follow-up questions.

How to play
  1. Choose a speaking time: 1, 3 or 5 minutes.
  2. Reveal any card and discuss the image or story.
  3. Start the timer for the student’s answer.
  4. Tap the heart when the story is finished and move to the next card.
Adults · A2–B1 · 10–20 minStart activity →

📸Your game image here
Prediction · Discussion

Instagram vs Reality

Twelve holiday situations for describing perfect expectations, predicting problems and revealing the funny reality.

How to play
  1. Choose a card or use Random card.
  2. Describe the expectation and predict what might go wrong.
  3. Reveal reality and compare it with your prediction.
  4. Rate how relatable it is and answer the personal questions.
Adults · A2–B1 · 15–25 minStart activity →
