First lessons after the break

Start with a real conversation

A warm collection of visual activities that helps learners reconnect,
share holiday stories and start speaking naturally from the very first minutes of the lesson.

Activity 01
Storytelling

Holiday Stories

Eight visual prompts for sharing summer memories, building longer answers and asking natural follow-up questions.

  1. Choose a speaking time: 1, 3 or 5 minutes.
  2. Reveal any card and discuss the image or story.
  3. Start the timer for the student’s answer.
  4. Tap the heart when the story is finished and move to the next card.
Start activity →
Activity 02
Prediction

Expectations vs Reality

Twelve holiday situations for describing perfect expectations, predicting problems and revealing the funny reality.

  1. Choose a card or use Random card.
  2. Describe the expectation and predict what might go wrong.
  3. Reveal reality and compare it with your prediction.
  4. Rate how relatable it is and answer the personal questions.
Start activity →
Activity 03
Choices

Travel Choices

Twelve travel dilemmas for making choices, explaining preferences and building a personal perfect-holiday story.

  1. Choose one of the two travel options.
  2. Turn the card and answer both follow-up questions.
  3. Continue through all 12 dilemmas and collect your choices.
  4. Use every choice to describe your perfect holiday.
